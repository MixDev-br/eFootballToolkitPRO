REQUIREMENTS AND ACTIVATION
- Windows 10/11 (64-bit), an internet connection and eFootball on Steam or Xbox PC.
- Extract the entire ZIP folder and run EFT_MATCH.exe as administrator.
- Use your exclusive EFTM-... license key linked to this PC.
  Toolkit keys do not activate EFT MATCH.
- Keep an internet connection available throughout use.
- EFT MATCH does not require Npcap.

HOW TO USE
1. Open eFootball and stay in the menu, without searching for a match yet.
2. Enter your own nickname exactly as shown in eFootball.
   On Xbox PC, use your in-game nickname, not your Xbox account gamertag.
3. Click Start monitor and wait for Ready to search.
4. Search for a new match. Available opponent information appears in the
   overlay. Unavailable fields display N/A.

PREFERENCES
- Drag the overlay header to move it. Use Hide to dismiss it and
  Show overlay again to reopen it.
- Choose a display duration between 20 seconds and 10 minutes.
  This controls only the overlay, not the length of the match.
- Your nickname, PT/EN language and preferences are saved automatically.
- The greeting uses the customer name linked to your license.
  The nickname field is still used to identify you in the game.
- Keep only one edition of eFootball running.
- Use borderless windowed mode if the overlay does not appear in exclusive fullscreen.

UPDATES
Keep EFT MATCH updated to continue receiving improvements and support.
Important improvements to security, stability and protection of internal components.
EFT MATCH checks for updates at startup and periodically during use.
You can also use the Updates button.
When an update is required, install the new version to continue using the
monitor. Your eFootball game will not be closed.

To update:
1. Click Download update and wait for it to finish.
2. Click Open folder and close.
3. Extract the entire new ZIP folder and run the new EFT_MATCH.exe.
Your activation and preferences are kept on this Windows user account.
If the download fails, check your connection and try again.
